Last updated: 19 November 2026

The AI landscape is dense, chaotic, and evolving exponentially. In this context, terms, concepts, and initiatives overlap and are frequently ambiguous, making it more difficult to build the shared understanding necessary to achieve interoperability and trust in agentic systems.The AI Knowledge Representation Community Group (AI KR CG) seeks to reduce the gaps in shared concepts and semantics. It monitors the evolution of AI across multiple dimensions — identifying, mapping, and analysing the primitives emerging from research and related standardization efforts. Using canonical knowledge-representation techniques, the AI KR CG builds shared, explicit representations — categories, taxonomies, and vocabularies — that support alignment, consistency, and model integrity across disparate initiatives, giving stakeholders common terms of reference for a fast-moving field.Through work on taxonomies, vocabularies, a standards inventory, gap analysis, and more, the Community Group hopes to help individuals and organisations stay up to date with developments in the field and achieve a deeper understanding of the ongoing evolution of the agentic-AI landscape

This repository holds shared notes and memos aimed at informing AI KR CG participants about the state of the art, through the representation and definition of key terms and concepts in the fast-moving agentic-AI landscape.

The content of these files is open for discussion, evaluation, and refinement. Because the space is emergent and the work is iterative, some of the older files may contain duplicate, missing, or redundant information that has since been merged into the newer files below. Nothing here is a W3C Standard or on the W3C Standards Track.

Participants are welcome to contact the chair, discuss via the public mailing list, or suggest how to develop these resources further.

Start here — the core resources

Three linked working drafts form the current spine of the group’s agentic-AI work. They share one structure — eight knowledge domains — and are the documents most in need of your review and contribution. All are working drafts, provisional and open for review.

  • Agentic AI Knowledge Domains — Taxonomy For Review
    W3C AI KR CG  •  Working draft
    The top-level category map: eight knowledge domains under which the vocabulary and the standards inventory are organised. Each term links to a current source; entries are provisional.
  • Agentic AI Vocabulary For Review
    W3C AI KR CG  •  Working draft
    A controlled vocabulary of ~150 agentic-AI terms with proposed definitions, sources, and SKOS-style relations, organised by the eight taxonomy categories, plus an acronym quick-reference.
  • Agentic AI Standards Inventory & Gap Analysis For Review
    W3C AI KR CG  •  Working draft
    An inventory of current agentic-AI standards and efforts with a per-domain coverage/gap assessment and recommended work items, organised by the eight taxonomy categories.

KR for Trust — current trust-layer work

Drafts arising from the trust-layer thread on public-aikr (opened 3 August 2026). All files live in the trust folder and are open for discussion; nothing here has been agreed by the group.

  • Knowledge Representation of the Trust Layer: Draft Community Group Report Open Consultation Status
    ReSpec report built from the technical note below, recording the trust-layer work so far and the opening of the Trust KR task force. Consultation closes 1 December 2026.
  • AI Agent Trust Record Draft
    One record per agent in thirteen fields: identity, interface, mandate and the parties who build, run and authorise it, with a trust assertion. Build a record, add it to the register, export it; a Mappings tab shows which agent trust protocols match each field. Larger-text and high-contrast modes. Defined by the draft visualisation specification.
  • Agent Trust Protocols and Specifications Explorer For Review
    Draft protocols and specifications for agent identity, authorisation and revocation, each read on a text-stage scale and an evidence scale, with links to the drafts. Checked 18 November 2026; corrections tagged [trustexplorer].
  • Agent Ontologies Explorer: AIAO, AgentO, MBAD Draft
    Three agent ontologies drawn side by side from their sources, with a draft crosswalk to the KR for Trust vocabulary.
  • Authority Continuity: a Formal Model and Proposed Cases Contribution
    Contributed by Nicola Gallo (PR #22). A threat model and proposed cases for the scope variant of case C9.
  • Key and Evidence States: Test Cases Contribution
    Runnable cases contributed by Kenne Ives (PR #25), with pass, fail or could-not-determine outcomes for key lifecycle situations, and an illustrated guide.
  • Agent Conformance Reporting Format v0.1 (pointer) Contribution
    Pointer to the Agent Conformance and Benchmarking CG reporting format edited by Evgenii Arsentev (PR #13); its Section 12 links the comparison semantics note.
  • DNS and DID Trust for Agents Note
    Draft for the CG report following DNS-anchored and DID-anchored agent trust.
  • Agent Protocol Primer Primer
    Newcomer's guide to the vocabulary used on the AI Agent Protocol CG list.
  • Knowledge Representation for Trust: Vocabulary, Use Cases and Working Draft For Review
    Use-case cards drawn from agent incidents and scenarios, a shared glossary, a trace from each weak point to proposed fixes and KRT terms, a coverage grid of contributors’ proposals, and plain-English summaries of the latest list exchanges. Proposed for discussion at the weekly calls from 21 September 2026.
  • KR for Trust Ontology (KRT), Skeleton v0.1 Draft
    Classes, properties, controlled values and SHACL validation rules R0–R11 for recording trust decisions involving AI agents. Files: krt.ttl (ontology), krt-shapes.ttl (shapes), krt-example.ttl (one passing and one failing record).
  • The Knowledge Representation of the Trust Layer Through the Lens of Digital Identity Chains Open Consultation Status
    Draft technical note on resolution dependency in agent identity, addressed to the W3C groups working on agent trust, with a survey of related efforts outside W3C and annexes on Base L2, MolTrust and the Agent Identity Protocol, and a section on resolution dependency as a PACELC position (PR #14, Ankur Chrungoo). Consultation opened 2 August 2026 and closes 1 December 2026.
  • Comparison Semantics Note
    Full text of §4.1 of the trust-layer note: why canonical bytes do not establish that two artefacts concern the same subject, the three failure classes (Unicode normalization, large integers, divergent declared semantics), and what a profile must state.
  • What the Agentic Web Actually Rests On: DNS Anchoring and the ARIA Public Surface Technical Note
    Findings from queries against the ARIA public verification surface (8–9 September 2026), set against the wider field of DNS-anchored agent identity proposals. Prepared for the TrustLayer Foundation and offered to the AI KR CG for discussion; open for correction.
  • Root KSK Rollover, W3C Trust Groups and AI KR Note
    The DNS root key change of 11 October 2026, how the Credentials CG is discussing DNSSEC-anchored DIDs, implications for the open and agentic web, open questions on risks to everyday users, and links to the related TPAC 2026 issues.
  • Agent Trust & Identity: Vocabulary Crosswalk Across W3C Community Groups Open Consultation Status
    A focused crosswalk of the identity/trust-specific W3C groups (Agent Trust Protocol CG, Agent Identity Registry Protocol CG, W3C Credentials CG, and related DID/VC work) converging on overlapping agent-identity vocabulary. Consultation closes 1 December 2026, with the trust note.
  • ARIA Terminology Disambiguation: WAI-ARIA vs. Agent-ARIA Open Consultation Status
    Disambiguates the accessibility WAI-ARIA vocabulary from the unrelated “Agent-ARIA” identity/authorization proposal circulating in agent-identity discussions — a naming collision worth flagging early. Consultation closes 1 December 2026, with the trust note. See also the companion plain-language explainer on agent identity and authorization.

Repository contents — reference material

Earlier and supporting notes. Several of these fed into the three core resources above; some information they contain has since been merged or superseded. Retained for reference and history.

  • W3C AI / Agentic-AI Vocabulary Crosswalk Open Consultation Status
    A crosswalk of current W3C Working Groups, Community Groups, and the Web & AI Interest Group whose scope touches AI or agentic AI — home page, repo, spec links, and main vocabulary terms for each, with notes flagging where groups are converging on overlapping terminology independently. Consultation closes 15 January 2027.

    Summary, ecosystem alignment notes and a SKOS-style glossary built from the draft Commission guidelines on high-risk classification under Article 6 of the AI Act; prepared for the CG’s response to the July 2026 targeted consultation.

    A ReSpec overview of the group’s purpose and how the documents in this repository fit together.

    An interactive board placing each effort by framework layer (representation → trust) and openness.
  • Agentic AI Standards Inventory (earlier) Superseded
    The earlier standards inventory; largely consolidated into the Standards Inventory & Gap Analysis in the core resources above.

  • Conduit Feedback Editor’s Draft

    Guidance on authoring specifications readable and consumable by AI agents and automated tools.
  • Accessibility and Sustainability Principles for AI Code Assistants Technical Note
