How the list was built

All 102 monthly subject indexes of public-aikr were downloaded and every thread read by subject. Threads that announce, share or circulate something made for or inside the group were opened and their first message read. Reading suggestions, calls for papers, news and other groups' announcements were left out.

The wiki was read through its main page and the pages it links. The repository was cloned with full history; dates for repository documents are the first commit.

Tags

  • Vocabulary covers term lists, glossaries, controlled vocabularies and term crosswalks.
  • Ontology means classes and properties with relations, formal or skeletal, and ontology alignments.
  • Taxonomy means a hierarchy or category scheme.
  • Other holds the rest, with the form named under the tag: reports, notes, StratML plans, specifications, schemas, concept maps, surveys.

Limits

Announced only marks items held in Google Drive, attachments or external sites that were not opened; the tag comes from the announcing message. Wiki special pages refuse automated access, so wiki pages not linked from the main page may be missing. Member contributions are listed because they were submitted to the group; filter them out for CG-only output.
