A representation of AI agent trust

One record per agent: who it is, where it acts, what it may do, who wrote it, who runs it, who authorised it, who owns and funds it. Agent type is described with the AIAO dimensions.

Schema

Fields, grouped

Eleven fields in five groups. Agent type takes one value per AIAO dimension, with cognitive capabilities listed as properties.

Descriptions in italics (protocol, authority, policy) are proposals still open for discussion in the W3C AI KR Community Group.
