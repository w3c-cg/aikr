What the selftest claims

  1. Expectation match — every expected row equals the checker, on all 18 cases.
  2. Permissive checker is caught — a checker that ignores content, folds any hold into pass, ignores staleness, and never reads the signer key or the lineage disagrees on 13 of 18 cases (D03, D05, D06, D07, D08, D10, D11, D12, D13, D14, D15, D17, D18).
  3. Tamper moves the result — D02 with an in-grant scope becomes pass.
  4. Bound is not silently widened — 86400 → 90000 flips D13 from record-unresolved to pass.
  5. Receipt requirement is live — D16's refusal without a receipt fails the receipt check; adding a bound receipt clears it.
  6. Citation must be walked — D18's cited digest added to the chain clears its unresolved verdict.
  7. Policy digest is reproducible — the digest is recomputed from policy.json itself and must equal the stored value.
  8. A hold names its side — record-only gives record-unresolved, checker-only gives check-incomplete, undeclared-scope-only gives absent, and all three together report each in unknown.record, unknown.absent and unknown.checker while the verdict stays on record-unresolved.
  9. Vector set matches what it states — 104 vectors carry their own expect block; any disagreement fails the run.
