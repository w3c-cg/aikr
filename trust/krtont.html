W3C AI Knowledge Representation Community Group, trust work

DRAFT, open for revision (0.2 draft for discussion (0.1 plus proposed additions, not adopted)). This vocabulary is a proposed skeleton. It is not a W3C Standard, not a Community Group Report and not adopted by the Community Group. Any class, property, label or definition may change or be removed.

Ongoing work needed. The vocabulary needs continuing maintenance: definitions for the 70 classes and properties that have none yet, and for controlled values without one, review of existing definitions against their sources, curation of missing terms, and consensus on the public-aikr list before any term is adopted. It is intended to be published as a specification once that work is done.

How to contribute. Propose definitions, missing terms or corrections as issues on w3c-cg/aikr with "KRT" and the term in the title, or on public-aikr@w3.org with [trust-kr] in the subject. This page is generated from krt.ttl: the Turtle file is the source, so changes go there.

About this vocabulary

Skeleton vocabulary for recording trust decisions involving AI agents: who presents a claim, on whose authority, with what evidence, for which action, enforced where, and with what outcome. Derived from case work on documented agent incidents and from the AIKR CG trust thread.
