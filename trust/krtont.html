DRAFT, open for revision (0.1 draft for discussion). This vocabulary is a proposed skeleton. It is not a W3C Standard, not a Community Group Report and not adopted by the Community Group. Any class, property, label or definition may change or be removed.

Ongoing work needed. The vocabulary needs continuing maintenance: definitions for the 61 terms that have none yet, review of existing definitions against their sources, curation of missing terms, and consensus on the public-aikr list before any term is adopted. It is intended to be published as a specification once that work is done.

How to contribute. Propose definitions, missing terms or corrections as issues on w3c-cg/aikr with "KRT" and the term in the title, or on public-aikr@w3.org with [trust-kr] in the subject. This page is generated from krt.ttl: the Turtle file is the source, so changes go there.

About this vocabulary

Skeleton vocabulary for recording trust decisions involving AI agents: who presents a claim, on whose authority, with what evidence, for which action, enforced where, and with what outcome. Derived from case work on documented agent incidents and from the AIKR CG trust thread.
