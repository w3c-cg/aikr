Fabian, Chairs and Participants
Thanks for the invitation to introduce ourselves

The web is of the most transformational technologies we have seen in of our lifetime, with incalculable impact on  humanity.
our lives depend on it .


It central architecture  gives the ability to publish information and knowledge and to search/find based on natural language keywords
in real time. This is invaluable.

This simple feature has delivered powerful capabilities and deep societal transformations through new services and tools

But the web has become overwhelmed, admittedly polluted by a lot of junk and discredited, with tons of misinformation and noise.

The architecture of the web itself is no longer clearcut as it once was with decentralization.

New distributed architectures changing the way files are stored and retrieved, search engines as we know them may become a thing of the past
In fact, the quality fo search results has already been challenged in recent years, requiring much higher precision to retrieve results
For how long will it be working?

The availability of LLMs and AI, Machine learning, neural networks, the web is a platform for scientific and technological change
For how long before ot becomes inaccessible?


New languages, terminologies, protocols, new possibilities and new risks
And who to ask what's happening? If not the W3C?

Agentic AI is delivering powerful capabilities and making the means of production more accessible,
they are also been used in malicious ways, to create agent trolls.  So everywhere on the web now there are trolling AI agents
ready to intervene and make sure users do not get to the knowledge they want.

The fact is, that even just the complexity of the landscape can point to the fact that the web itself
is shifting from a realm of endless possibilities, to a risky minefield just to be on

So hands off to everyone who works robustly to make the web accessible, safe and maintaining it its best possible shape

We love the web the way it was,  many say 'can we have the old web back'

The vision at AI KR CG is to leverage the power of agentic AI in every possible way to make
sure that the world wide web does not get buried in inextricable layer of complexity with a minefield of hidden
vulnerabilities

Explicit, shared, findable knowledge remains central critical feature.  The Agentic Web can develop to serve
that purpose, or against it
